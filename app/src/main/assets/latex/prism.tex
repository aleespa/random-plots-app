\text{Prism extends Andrej Bauer's Random Art. A random}\\
\text{formula assigns each point \((x,y)\) one value}\\
\[
f(x,y) \in [-1, 1]
\]\\
\text{which is coloured through the chosen palette.}\\
\text{Besides waves, folds and mixes, the formula can}\\
\text{measure distances to random focal points \(p_i\),}\\
\[
d(x,y) = \min_i \lVert (x,y) - p_i \rVert
\]\\
\text{and evaluate whole sub-formulas at transformed}\\
\text{coordinates: rotations, mirrors, \(n\)-fold}\\
\text{kaleidoscopes and tilings. Parts of the formula}\\
\text{are reused in several places, so the same motif}\\
\text{reappears under different symmetries.}
